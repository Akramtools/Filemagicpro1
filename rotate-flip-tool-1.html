How to Rotate or Flip a PDF/Image

1. Upload your PDF or image (JPG, PNG, WebP) by clicking or dragging it into the box above.

2. Use Rotate 90°, 180°, or Flip Horizontal/Vertical to transform it. For PDFs, choose whether to apply the change to all pages or just the one you're viewing.

3. Click Download to save the result — nothing is ever uploaded to a server.

Why Use This Tool?

Rotating scanned documents or sideways photos is a common problem, and most tools force you to upload files to a server. This one runs entirely in your browser: PDF rotation preserves the original vector quality, and flipping (mirroring) is handled with a high-resolution image fallback only on the pages that need it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I rotate a PDF online for free?
Upload your PDF, click Rotate 90°, 180°, or 270°, choose whether to apply it to all pages or just the current one, then download the rotated file. It all happens in your browser.
Can I flip or mirror a PDF page?
Yes, use the Flip Horizontal or Flip Vertical buttons. Flipping a PDF page saves that page as a high quality image internally, since true mirroring isn't a native PDF rotation.
Does rotating a PDF reduce its quality?
No, pure rotation keeps the original PDF vector quality intact. Only pages you flip (mirror) are converted to a high-resolution image.
Can I rotate or flip an image too?
Yes, this tool works for both PDF files and images like JPG, PNG, and WebP, with the same rotate and flip controls.
Is my file uploaded to any server?
No, everything runs locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your file never leaves your device.
Is this tool free to use?
Yes, completely free with no signup, watermark, or usage limits.
Which image formats are supported?
JPG, PNG, and WebP images are supported, along with standard PDF files.
Can I rotate just one page of a multi-page PDF?
Yes, uncheck "Apply changes to all pages" and navigate to the specific page you want to rotate or flip before clicking the buttons.
