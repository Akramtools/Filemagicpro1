How to Sign a PDF Online

1. Upload your PDF using the box above.

2. Draw your signature on the pad with your finger or mouse.

3. Choose the page and position, then click "Sign PDF & Download" — your signed file downloads instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign a PDF online for free?
Upload your PDF, draw your signature on the pad, position it on the page using the sliders, then download the signed PDF.
Is my file uploaded to any server?
No, the signature is drawn and placed locally in your browser. Your file never leaves your device.
Can I sign on more than one page?
Currently the signature is placed on the page you select. Repeat the process for additional pages by re-uploading if needed.
Is this legally binding?
This tool creates a visual signature image on your PDF. For legally binding e-signatures with audit trails, consult a certified e-signature service.
