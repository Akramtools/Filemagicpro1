Why Our AI Converter Never Produces Blank PDFs

Unlike other client-side tools that rely on complex canvas rendering, FileMagic Pro uses the browser’s native print-to-PDF engine (the same one behind "Ctrl+P" > "Save as PDF"). This guarantees that every element – including nested tables, images, headers, footers, and fonts – appears exactly as designed. If you can see the preview, you get a perfect PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it support .doc files?
No, only .docx (modern Word format). Open your .doc in Word or Google Docs and save as .docx first.
Why is my PDF blank?
With this new version, blank pages cannot happen. If preview shows content, PDF will be perfect. Make sure you uploaded a valid .docx file.
My images or tables are missing?
Mammoth extracts images, and print engine renders them. For complex vector graphics, convert them to PNG inside Word first.
