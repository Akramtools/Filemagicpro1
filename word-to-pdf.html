How AI Word to PDF Works

Our AI engine uses Mammoth.js to intelligently analyze your Word document's structure (headings, paragraphs, tables, lists, images) and converts it to clean HTML while preserving all formatting. Then html2pdf.js renders that HTML into a high-fidelity PDF directly in your browser. No server upload, no data leakage.

Why Choose FileMagic Pro AI Converter?

Unlike basic converters that only extract plain text, our AI retains complex layouts, nested tables, bullet points, numbered lists, embedded images, font styles, and even basic colors. The result is a PDF that looks exactly like the original Word file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it support .doc (old Word) files?
Only .docx format is supported because modern AI conversion libraries work natively with Office Open XML. Please open your .doc file in Word or Google Docs and save as .docx before converting.
Will my images and tables show up correctly?
Yes! Images are embedded, tables keep their borders and alignment, and lists are preserved. Complex vector graphics may simplify, but standard content works great.
Is my data sent to any server?
Absolutely not. The entire conversion happens inside your browser. Your file never leaves your device.
Can I convert password-protected Word files?
No, remove the password first using Word or Google Docs before uploading.
