Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert an image format online for free?
Upload your image, choose the output format (JPG, PNG or WEBP), optionally resize or adjust quality, then download.
Can I convert PNG to JPG and lose the transparency?
Yes, converting PNG to JPG fills transparent areas with white since JPG does not support transparency.
Is my image uploaded to any server?
No, conversion happens locally in your browser using the canvas API. Your image never leaves your device.
Can I resize the image while converting?
Yes, enter a target width and the height scales automatically to keep the aspect ratio.
