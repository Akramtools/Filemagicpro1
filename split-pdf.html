How to Split a PDF Online

1. Upload your PDF file using the box above.

2. Choose "Split All Pages" to get every page as a separate PDF (downloaded as a ZIP), or "Extract Range" to pull out specific pages into one file.

3. Click Split PDF — your download starts automatically, and nothing is ever sent to a server.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I split a PDF online for free?
Upload your PDF, choose to split every page into a separate file or extract a specific page range, then download the result as a ZIP or PDF.
Can I extract just a few pages from a PDF?
Yes, switch to Extract Range mode, enter the page numbers you want (e.g. 2-5), and download a single PDF with only those pages.
Is my file uploaded to any server?
No, splitting happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your file never leaves your device.
Is there a page limit?
No artificial limit, though very large PDFs depend on your device's memory since everything runs in-browser.
What format do I get back?
Splitting into individual pages gives you a ZIP of PDFs. Extracting a range gives you one PDF file.
