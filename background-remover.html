Frequently Asked Questions
📤 Is my image uploaded to any server?▼
No. Everything runs 100% in your browser using JavaScript and WebGL. Your image never leaves your device — not even for a millisecond. This is completely private and secure.
🐢 Why is the first run slow?▼
The AI model (~10MB) needs to download once from Google's CDN the very first time. After that it gets cached in your browser and runs instantly on every subsequent use — even offline.
📷 What images give the best results?▼
Best results on: people/portraits, product photos, and subjects with clear contrast against their background. Tips for better output: use good lighting, avoid busy backgrounds, and try adjusting the Threshold slider if edges look rough.
🖼️ What format is the output file?▼
By default you get a PNG with transparent background — perfect for use in designs, presentations, and documents. You can also choose a solid replacement background (white, blue, black etc.) and download as JPG.
📱 Does it work on Android and iPhone?▼
Yes! Works on Chrome, Samsung Browser, Safari, Firefox and all modern mobile browsers. No special settings or app download needed. Just open the page and use it.
🆓 Is this tool completely free?▼
Yes, 100% free — forever. No account required, no watermarks added, no daily limits, no hidden charges. FileMagic Pro is always free for all users.
🎨 Can I change the background color after removing?▼
Yes! In the Settings panel, use the "Replace Background" dropdown to choose White, Light Blue, Black, Gray, or any custom color you want. The change is applied instantly before downloading.
📏 What is the maximum image size?▼
Maximum file size is 20MB. Very large images (above 4000×4000 px) are automatically scaled down to 1024px for faster AI processing, then the result is rendered at a good quality resolution.
⚙️ What is the Threshold slider for?▼
The Threshold controls how strictly the AI separates foreground from background. Higher value = keeps more of the subject (good for detailed hair/fur). Lower value = removes more aggressively. Adjust it if you see too much background remaining or if the subject is being cut off.
🤖 What AI model does this use?▼
This tool uses MediaPipe Selfie Segmentation — a lightweight AI model developed by Google, optimized for real-time browser performance. It works without needing any API key or server connection.
🌄 General vs Landscape model — what's the difference?▼
General model (Model 0) is optimized for close-up shots — portraits, selfies, product photos. Landscape model (Model 1) works better for full-body shots and wider scenes. Try both and see which gives better results for your image.
🪪 Can I use this for passport photos?▼
Yes! Remove background and then select "White" or "Light Blue" as the replacement color. Then use our Passport Photo Maker tool to create a proper passport photo sheet.
