How to Compress Images Online Free

Select your JPG or PNG image, adjust the quality slider to your preference, and the compressed image is shown instantly alongside the original. When you are happy, click Download. The entire process happens in your browser with no data sent to any server.

Why Compress Images?

Smaller image files load faster on websites, take up less storage, and are easier to share over WhatsApp or email. FileMagic Pro lets you control the quality-to-size trade-off with a simple slider, so you always get the right balance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What formats are supported?
JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP images are supported, both as input and output. You can convert between formats while compressing.
What's the maximum file size?
You can compress images from a few KB up to 300MB. Very large images are automatically resized to a safe maximum dimension so the tool stays smooth on phones and low-memory devices.
What is "Target File Size" mode?
Instead of guessing a quality percentage, enter your desired output size (e.g. 100 KB) and the tool automatically finds the best quality and dimensions to get as close as possible to that size.
What quality setting should I use?
80% is ideal for web use — great visual quality with significantly smaller file size. For maximum compression, use 50–60% or switch to WebP format.
Is my image uploaded anywhere?
No. Compression happens entirely in your browser using the Canvas API. Your images never leave your device, even for very large files.
Will the compressed image have a watermark?
No watermarks, ever. Your output image is clean.
