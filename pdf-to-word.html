How to Convert PDF to Word Online Free

Simply select your PDF file, click Convert to Word, and FileMagic Pro extracts all the text from every page of your PDF and packages it into a downloadable Word document. Open the result in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or LibreOffice to edit freely.

Why Convert PDF to Word?

PDFs are great for sharing but hard to edit. Converting a PDF to Word lets you modify text, change formatting, copy sections, and repurpose the content. FileMagic Pro makes this conversion free and instant, with no cloud upload required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the Word file keep my PDF formatting?
The output preserves the text content with basic structure — headings and paragraphs. Complex layouts like columns and tables may simplify, as browser-based conversion works with extracted text rather than layout data.
Can I edit the Word file after downloading?
Yes! Open it in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or LibreOffice Writer. All text is fully editable.
Is my PDF uploaded to your server?
No. Everything runs in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes! FileMagic Pro works on Android and iOS browsers. The downloaded file opens in any office app.
What about scanned PDFs?
Scanned PDFs are images — text cannot be extracted without OCR. This tool works best with digitally created PDFs.
