Frequently Asked Questions

How is attendance percentage calculated?
Attendance Percentage = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes Held) × 100. For example, if you attended 60 out of 80 classes, your attendance is 75%.
How many classes can I skip and still maintain 75%?
It depends on your current attended classes, total classes, and how many classes remain in the term. This calculator tells you exactly how many more classes you can miss while still meeting your target percentage.
What is the minimum attendance required in Indian colleges?
Most Indian universities require a minimum of 75% attendance, though some allow 65% with valid medical or documented reasons. Always check your specific college or university rules.
What happens if my attendance falls below the required percentage?
Most colleges either debar students from sitting the final exam or require a condonation application with valid supporting documents, depending on institution rules.
Can I recover my attendance percentage later?
Yes, by attending all remaining classes without missing any. Use the "Classes Remaining" field above to see exactly how many you must attend to reach your target.
Does this work for school and college both?
Yes, this calculator works for any attendance scenario — schools, colleges, universities, or coaching institutes — since it only needs classes attended, total classes, and your required percentage.
