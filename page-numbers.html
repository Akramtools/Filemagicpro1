Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add page numbers to a PDF online for free?
Upload your PDF, choose the position and starting number, then download the PDF with page numbers added to every page.
Can I start numbering from a page other than 1?
Yes, set a custom starting number and every page will count up from there.
Is my file uploaded to any server?
No, page numbers are added locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your file never leaves your device.
Can I choose where the number appears?
Yes, pick bottom-center, bottom-right, or top-right placement.
