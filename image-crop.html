How to Crop an Image

1. Upload your image by clicking or dragging it into the box above.

2. Optionally tap a preset aspect ratio, or leave it Free for any shape.

3. Drag the crop box to move it, or drag a corner handle to resize — then click Crop & Download.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I crop an image online?
Upload your image, drag the crop box to select the area you want, optionally lock to a preset aspect ratio, then click Crop & Download.
What aspect ratio presets are available?
Free (any shape), Square (1:1), 4:3, 16:9, 3:2, and Story/Portrait (9:16) are all available as one-tap presets.
Can I resize the crop box after placing it?
Yes, drag any corner handle to resize the crop box, and drag inside it to move it around the image.
Will cropping reduce image quality?
No, the cropped area is extracted at its original resolution with no compression loss.
Is my photo uploaded to a server?
No, cropping happens entirely in your browser using the Canvas API. Your photo is never uploaded anywhere.
What format is the cropped image?
The cropped image downloads as a PNG file to preserve full quality.
