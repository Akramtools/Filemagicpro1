How to Pick Colors from an Image

1. Upload your image by clicking or dragging it into the box above.

2. Tap or click anywhere on the image to pick that exact pixel's color.

3. A dominant color palette is automatically generated below — tap any swatch to copy its HEX code.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pick a color from an image?
Upload your image, then tap or click anywhere on it. The exact HEX and RGB code of that pixel appears instantly, along with a copy button.
How does the palette extractor work?
The tool analyzes all pixels in the image and groups similar colors together to find the most dominant and representative colors, displayed as a palette.
Can I copy the color codes?
Yes, tap any color swatch or the picked color box to copy its HEX code to your clipboard instantly.
Does this tool upload my image anywhere?
No, all color extraction happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your image is never uploaded to any server.
What image formats are supported?
JPG, PNG, and WebP images are supported.
Can I use this for choosing brand or design colors?
Yes, it's commonly used by designers to pull exact colors from photos, logos, or mockups for use in design tools.
