Frequently Asked Questions
What does "indexed" mean? +
When Google indexes a page, it means Google has visited, read, and stored that page in its database. Only indexed pages can appear in Google Search results.
My page shows "Not Indexed" — what should I do? +
Go to Google Search Console → URL Inspection → enter your URL → click "Request Indexing". Google will crawl and index your page within a few days.
How accurate is this tool? +
This tool uses Google's own site: operator which is the standard way to check indexing. It is highly accurate but may have a slight delay vs Google's internal data.

Can I check competitor websites too? +
Yes! You can check any public URL on the internet — your site, competitor sites, or any webpage you want to verify.
