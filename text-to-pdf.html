How to Convert Text to PDF Online

1. Type or paste your text into the box, or upload a .txt file.

2. Choose font size and page size.

3. Click "Convert to PDF & Download" — the PDF is generated instantly in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert text to PDF online for free?
Type or paste your text into the box, choose font size and page size, then click Convert to download a PDF.
Can I upload a .txt file instead of typing?
Yes, use the upload option to load a .txt file directly into the text box before converting.
Is my text uploaded to any server?
No, the PDF is generated locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.
Does it support Hindi or other languages?
This tool works best with standard Latin-script text. For Hindi/Devanagari documents, use the OCR PDF tool instead.
