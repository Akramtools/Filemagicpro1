How to Convert Word to PDF Online Free

Select your DOC or DOCX file, choose your page size and orientation, and click Convert to PDF. FileMagic Pro reads your Word document in the browser, extracts the content, and creates a clean PDF file ready for download — all without uploading anything to a server.

Why Convert Word to PDF?

PDFs look the same on every device and cannot be accidentally edited. Sending resumes, reports, invoices, and proposals as PDF ensures your formatting stays intact for the recipient. Converting Word to PDF is one of the most common document tasks for students, professionals, and businesses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it support both .doc and .docx files?
Yes! Both older .doc format and newer .docx format are fully supported.
Will my Word formatting appear in the PDF?
Headings, paragraphs, and bold/italic text are preserved. Complex elements like tables and images may simplify slightly in browser-based conversion.
Is there a page or file size limit?
No server-side limits. Large documents may take a few extra seconds depending on your device speed.
Is my Word document uploaded anywhere?
No. Your file never leaves your browser. The entire conversion happens locally on your device.
Will the PDF have a watermark?
No watermarks. Your PDF is clean and professional.
