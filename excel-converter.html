Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Excel to PDF online for free?
Upload your XLSX or CSV file, choose PDF, HTML or JSON as the output, then download the converted file.
Does it support multiple sheets?
The first sheet is converted by default. Select a different sheet from the dropdown if your file has more than one.
Is my spreadsheet uploaded to any server?
No, conversion happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your file never leaves your device.
Can I convert CSV files too?
Yes, both .xlsx and .csv files are supported for upload.
