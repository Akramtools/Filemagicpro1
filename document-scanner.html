How to Scan a Document Online Free

Open the camera or upload an existing photo, and the scanner automatically finds the document's edges and straightens the perspective so it looks flat and front-facing. Drag the corner handles to fine-tune the crop, or switch to Rectangle Crop for a simple free-form box. Pick a filter, keep scanning more pages, then export everything as one PDF or as separate images — all inside your browser, nothing uploaded.

Why Use a Browser-Based Document Scanner?

A dedicated scanner app takes storage space and often asks for an account or shows ads. FileMagic Pro's scanner works instantly in any mobile or desktop browser, detects and straightens document edges the same way premium scanning apps do, and keeps every photo and page entirely on your device — nothing is ever sent to a server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my document uploaded to your server?
No. Everything runs in your browser — capture, edge detection, cropping, filters and PDF creation. Your files never leave your device.
Does it auto-detect the document edges?
Yes, it finds the document's four corners and straightens the perspective automatically. You can drag the corner handles to fine-tune it.
What if I want a simple rectangular crop instead?
Switch to Rectangle Crop mode for a free-form crop box with resizable edges and corners — no perspective warping.
Can I zoom in and out while scanning or cropping?
Yes, pinch with two fingers or use the on-screen plus/minus buttons on both the camera view and the crop screen — zoom in and out both work reliably.
Can I scan multiple pages into one PDF?
Yes, scan as many pages as you like, drag to reorder, delete any page, then export the whole set as a single PDF.
What filters are available?
Original color, Grayscale, high-contrast Black & White for text pages, and Enhance for automatic brightness/contrast correction.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes, it works on Android and iOS browsers using your phone camera, or you can upload a photo from your gallery.
Is there a page limit?
No fixed limit — keep adding pages before exporting the final PDF.
