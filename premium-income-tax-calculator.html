Frequently Asked Questions
Common questions about income tax in India
Is this calculator using the latest tax slabs? ▼
Yes, this calculator uses the New Tax Regime slabs for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27): 0-4L nil, 4-8L at 5%, 8-12L at 10%, 12-16L at 15%, 16-20L at 20%, 20-24L at 25%, and above 24L at 30%.
Is income up to ₹12 lakh really tax-free? ▼
Yes, under Section 87A, taxpayers with taxable income up to ₹12 lakh get a rebate that brings their tax liability to zero. Salaried individuals get this benefit up to ₹12.75 lakh after the ₹75,000 standard deduction.
What is the standard deduction? ▼
Salaried individuals and pensioners get a flat ₹75,000 standard deduction from gross income under the new tax regime, reducing their taxable income before slab rates are applied.
Does this calculator support the old tax regime? ▼
No, this version calculates tax only under the New Tax Regime, which is the default regime for most taxpayers. The old regime involves many deductions (80C, HRA, etc.) that vary case-by-case.
Is this official tax advice? ▼
No. This tool provides an estimate for informational purposes only. For actual tax filing, please consult a qualified chartered accountant or tax professional.

How Is Tax Calculated Under the New Regime?

Tax is calculated progressively across income slabs — each portion of your income is taxed at the rate for that slab, not your entire income at the highest applicable rate. A Section 87A rebate then makes the final tax zero for taxable income up to ₹12 lakh, and a 4% health and education cess is added on top of the computed tax.
