Free PDF OCR & Search Tool — English, Hindi, Marathi & Urdu

Scanned PDFs — old government forms, enumeration sheets, printed records — often can't be searched or copied because they're really just page images. This tool runs OCR (optical character recognition) directly in your browser, detecting pages that already have selectable text and skipping them for speed, then recognizing text on scanned pages using Tesseract.js with support for English, Hindi, Marathi, and Urdu. The result is a searchable PDF with an invisible text layer laid exactly over the original page image, so the document still looks the same but every word becomes findable and copyable. Nothing is uploaded to a server; the entire process, from reading the PDF to building the searchable output, happens on your device.

How Exact Search and OCR-Confusion Suggestions Work

Once OCR finishes, every page's recognized text is indexed for instant search. The search box performs an exact match first — useful for finding a specific EPIC number, a single Marathi letter, or a name spelled a particular way. If nothing matches exactly, the tool checks a small set of characters that OCR commonly confuses, such as O and 0, I and 1, or S and 5, and shows those as clearly labeled possible matches rather than exact results. This two-step approach avoids false confidence: you always know whether a result is a confirmed exact match or a suggestion worth double-checking against the original scan.

Choosing a Processing Mode and Editing Results

Fast mode skips pages that already have selectable text and renders scanned pages at a lower quality for quicker OCR — a good default for long documents. Balanced and High Accuracy render scanned pages at higher resolution before recognition, which improves accuracy on small or low-contrast text at the cost of more processing time. After OCR completes, the built-in editor lets you correct misread names, numbers, or Marathi characters page by page — these corrections update the searchable text index immediately, so search results reflect your fixes even though the visible scanned image itself stays unchanged.
