How to Create a Favicon

1. Upload a square logo or image (it will be center-cropped if not square).

3. Download the ZIP, upload the files to your website, and paste the HTML code into your page's <head> section.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a favicon from an image?
Upload any square image or logo, and the tool automatically generates all standard favicon sizes plus the HTML code to paste into your site's head section.
What favicon sizes are generated?
16x16, 32x32, 48x48, 180x180 (Apple Touch Icon), 192x192 and 512x512 (Android/PWA) are all generated together.
Do I need a square image?
A square image works best. If your image isn't square, it will be center-cropped automatically to fit.
How do I add the favicon to my website?
Download the ZIP, upload the images to your site's root folder, then paste the provided HTML code into the <head> section of your pages.
Is my image uploaded to a server?
No, all favicon generation happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your image is never uploaded anywhere.
What file format are the favicons?
Favicons are generated as PNG files, which are supported by all modern browsers and devices.
