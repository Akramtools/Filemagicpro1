Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enhance a photo online for free?
Upload your image, adjust brightness, contrast, saturation and sharpness with the sliders, and download the enhanced version.
Is my photo uploaded to any server?
No, all enhancement happens locally using the browser canvas. Your image never leaves your device.
What image formats are supported?
JPG, PNG and WEBP images are supported for both upload and download.
Can I undo my changes?
Yes, use the Reset button to return all sliders to their original values.
