Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert a PDF to PowerPoint?
Upload your PDF, choose a slide quality, and click Convert to PPT. Each page becomes a full-slide image in the downloaded PPTX file.
Will the text be editable in PowerPoint after conversion?
No, each page is converted as a high-quality image to preserve the exact visual layout. The text itself is not editable, similar to how most free PDF-to-PPT tools work.
Why is the output an image instead of editable text and shapes?
Rebuilding editable text boxes, fonts, and shapes from a PDF requires complex layout analysis that isn't reliably possible in a browser. Converting pages as images guarantees the slides look exactly like the original PDF.
Is my PDF uploaded to a server?
No, the entire conversion happens locally in your browser using PDF.js. Your file is never uploaded anywhere.
Can I choose specific pages to convert?
Yes, you can enter a page range like 1-5 or specific pages like 1,3,5 before converting.
What slide size is used?
Slides are generated in standard 16:9 widescreen format with the PDF page centered and fitted to the slide.
