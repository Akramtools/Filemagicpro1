Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about merging PDFs
Is this PDF merger really free? ▼
Yes, 100% free. No hidden charges, no signup required, no watermarks added to your output PDF. FileMagic Pro is completely free to use.
Are my PDF files uploaded to a server? ▼
No. All processing happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your files never leave your device. This means your data is 100% private and secure.
How many PDFs can I merge at once? ▼
There is no strict limit on the number of files. However, since everything runs in your browser, very large files or a very high number of files may slow down older devices. For best performance, try to keep total size under 200MB.
Can I change the order of pages before merging? ▼
Yes! After adding your files, you can drag and drop them to reorder, or use the up/down arrow buttons. The merge will follow the order you set in the list.
Will my merged PDF lose quality? ▼
No. The merge process combines PDFs at the document level without re-rendering or compressing content. Text, images, and formatting are preserved exactly as in the originals.
Does this work on mobile (Android/iPhone)? ▼
Yes! FileMagic Pro is fully optimized for mobile browsers including Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone. No app download needed.

Our tool uses the PDF-Lib JavaScript library to read and combine your PDF files locally on your device. This means zero privacy risk — your documents stay on your phone or computer at all times. The merged output is generated in memory and downloaded directly to your device.
