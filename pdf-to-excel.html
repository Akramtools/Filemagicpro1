How to Convert PDF to Excel

1. Upload your PDF file by clicking or dragging it into the box above.

2. Optionally specify a page range — otherwise all pages are converted.

3. Click Convert to Excel. Each PDF page becomes a separate sheet in the downloaded .xlsx file.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert a PDF to Excel?
Upload your PDF, choose which pages to convert, and click Convert to Excel. Each page becomes a separate sheet in the downloaded XLSX file.
Will the table formatting be preserved exactly?
The tool groups text by its position on the page to rebuild rows and columns. Simple tables convert well, but complex layouts with merged cells or multiple columns may need manual adjustment after conversion.
Does this work on scanned PDFs?
No, this tool reads the actual text layer in the PDF. For scanned or image-based PDFs, use an OCR PDF tool first to add a text layer.
Is my PDF uploaded to a server?
No, the entire conversion happens locally in your browser. Your file is never uploaded anywhere.
Can I convert only specific pages?
Yes, you can enter a page range like 1-3 or specific pages like 1,3,5 before converting.
What Excel format is the output?
The output is a standard .xlsx file that opens in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, or LibreOffice Calc.
