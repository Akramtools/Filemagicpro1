Frequently Asked Questions
Common questions about GST calculation
How is GST calculated? ▼
GST is calculated as a percentage of the base price: GST Amount = Base Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100. The total payable amount is the base amount plus the GST amount.
What is the difference between CGST and SGST? ▼
For transactions within the same state, GST is split equally between CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST). For example, an 18% GST rate becomes 9% CGST + 9% SGST.
What is IGST? ▼
IGST (Integrated GST) applies to inter-state transactions — sales between different states. The full GST rate is charged as IGST instead of being split into CGST and SGST.
How do I remove GST from a final price? ▼
Use the "Remove GST" mode and enter the final price including GST. The calculator extracts the base price using the formula: Base = Final Amount ÷ (1 + GST Rate/100).
What are the current GST slabs in India? ▼
India's main GST slabs are 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%, with some goods attracting 0% or special rates. The applicable rate depends on the category of goods or services.
