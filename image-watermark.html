How to Add a Watermark

1. Upload your image, then choose Text or Logo watermark mode.

2. Set position, opacity, size, and optionally enable Tile Pattern to repeat it across the image.

3. Preview updates live — click Download when ready.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a watermark to my photo?
Upload your image, choose text or logo watermark, customize position/opacity/size, then download the watermarked image.
Can I use my own logo as a watermark?
Yes, switch to Logo mode and upload a PNG with a transparent background for best results.
Can I tile the watermark across the whole image?
Yes, enable Tile Pattern to repeat the watermark diagonally across the entire image, which makes it harder to crop out.
Will the watermark reduce image quality?
No, the original image resolution is preserved. Only the watermark overlay is added on top.
Is my photo uploaded to a server?
No, the watermark is applied entirely in your browser using the Canvas API. Your photo is never uploaded anywhere.
What image format is the output?
The watermarked image downloads as a PNG file to preserve quality and any transparency.
