Extract Text from PDF — All Languages Supported

FileMagic Pro's PDF to Text tool uses PDF.js for accurate text extraction. It fully supports Unicode — meaning Hindi (Devanagari), Marathi, Arabic, Urdu, Bengali, Tamil, and all other scripts are extracted correctly without any garbled characters.

How to Convert PDF to Text Online

Simply upload your PDF, choose your options, and click Extract Text. The tool reads embedded text from your PDF pages — no OCR needed for digital PDFs. For scanned PDFs, text may not be extractable as they contain images only.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it support Hindi and Marathi PDFs? ▼
Yes! Full Unicode support means Hindi (हिंदी) and Marathi (मराठी) text is extracted correctly. The output preserves Devanagari script perfectly.
Does it support Arabic PDFs? ▼
Yes. Arabic (العربية) and Urdu (اردو) are supported. Enable RTL mode in Text Direction for best results with right-to-left languages.
Why is my text garbled or missing? ▼
This happens with scanned PDFs (images of text). Scanned PDFs need OCR software. Our tool works best with digital PDFs that have embedded text.
Is the tool free? ▼
Yes, completely free. No signup, no watermarks, no limits. Your file never leaves your device.
