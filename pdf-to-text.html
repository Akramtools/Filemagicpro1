Frequently Asked Questions

Can it extract text from scanned PDFs?
This tool extracts digitally embedded text. Scanned PDFs (which are images) require OCR software which is beyond browser capabilities.
Will it extract text from all pages?
Yes! All pages are processed and the text is combined in order.
Is there a page limit?
No page limit. However, very large PDFs may take a moment to process depending on your device speed.
Is my PDF secure?
Yes. Your file never leaves your device. All extraction happens locally in your browser.
