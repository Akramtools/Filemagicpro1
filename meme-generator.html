How to Make a Meme

1. Upload any image by clicking or dragging it into the box above.

2. Edit the default Top/Bottom text, or add more text boxes with the Add Text button.

3. Drag text directly on the image to reposition it, adjust size/color, then download.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a meme online?
Upload any image, type your top and bottom text (or add custom text boxes), adjust font size and position, then download your meme instantly.
Is there a watermark on the downloaded meme?
No, memes download completely clean with no watermark.
Can I move the text anywhere on the image?
Yes, drag any text box to reposition it anywhere on the image before downloading.
Can I add more than two text boxes?
Yes, use the Add Text button to add as many custom text boxes as you need.
Is my image uploaded to a server?
No, the entire meme is created locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your image is never uploaded anywhere.
What image format is the downloaded meme?
Memes download as a PNG file, which supports crisp text and works everywhere.
