Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a photo collage online?
Upload 2 to 9 photos, choose a grid layout that matches your photo count, adjust spacing and border color, then download your collage.
How many photos can I combine?
You can combine between 2 and 9 photos, with a matching grid layout automatically suggested.
Can I reorder the photos in the collage?
Yes, drag any photo thumbnail to reorder it before generating the collage.
Can I adjust spacing between photos?
Yes, use the spacing slider to control the gap between photos, and choose a border color.
Is my photo uploaded to a server?
No, the collage is created entirely in your browser using the Canvas API. Your photos are never uploaded anywhere.
What format is the downloaded collage?
The collage downloads as a high-quality PNG image.
