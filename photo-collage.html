How to Make a Photo Collage

1. Add photos, text, shapes or stickers using the toolbar.

2. Drag any element to move it, pull the corner dot to resize, and the top dot to rotate.

3. Tap an element to edit its filter, color, font or opacity, or use Auto Collage for an instant grid.

4. Pick a background, choose your canvas size, and tap Download to save your PNG.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a photo collage online?
Add photos, then drag, resize and rotate them freely on the canvas. Use Auto Collage for an instant grid, or arrange everything yourself, then add text, shapes or stickers before downloading.
Can I add text and shapes to my collage?
Yes, tap Text to add editable captions with custom fonts, colors and outlines, or tap Shape or Sticker to add rectangles, circles, stars, hearts and emoji stickers.
Can I apply filters to photos?
Yes, select any photo and choose from filters like grayscale, sepia, vintage, warm, cool, vivid and fade.
Can I change the background?
Yes, choose a solid color, a custom gradient, or a pattern like dots, stripes, grid, checker or confetti.
What canvas sizes are supported?
Square post, Instagram Story/Reel, landscape, A4 print, and a fully custom size.
Is my photo uploaded to a server?
No, the collage is created entirely in your browser using the Canvas API. Your photos are never uploaded anywhere.
What format is the downloaded collage?
The collage downloads as a high-resolution PNG image at your chosen canvas size.
