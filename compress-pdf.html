Frequently Asked Questions

How much can PDF be compressed? ▼
Typically 40–90% reduction depending on content. PDFs with many images compress more. Text-only PDFs compress less.
Is my PDF safe? ▼
Yes. Your file never leaves your device. All processing happens in your browser using JavaScript — completely private.
Which PDF files compress best? ▼
Scanned PDFs and image-heavy PDFs compress the most. Text-only PDFs may have less reduction.
What is the maximum file size? ▼
There is no hard limit. Very large files (100MB+) may take longer depending on your device speed.
Do I need to install any app? ▼
No. FileMagic Pro works directly in your mobile or desktop browser — no app, no extension, no signup required.
Will compressing reduce text quality? ▼
Text remains sharp at all compression levels since pages are rendered at a clear resolution before re-encoding — only the underlying image data is compressed.
