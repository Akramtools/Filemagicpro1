How to Compress PDF Online for Free

Select your PDF file, choose your compression level, and click Compress. FileMagic Pro reduces your PDF file size by optimizing internal structures and compressing embedded content. The result is a smaller PDF that retains readable text and acceptable image quality.

When Do You Need to Compress a PDF?

Large PDF files can be difficult to email, upload, or share. Compressing your PDF makes it easier to attach in emails (most limits are 10–25 MB), upload to portals, and store on your device. FileMagic Pro gives you control over how much compression to apply.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will compressing reduce the quality of my PDF?
Low compression barely affects quality. Medium is ideal for most uses. High compression gives the smallest file size and may reduce image sharpness slightly.
Is there a file size limit?
No server-side limits since everything runs in your browser. Very large PDFs (100MB+) may be slower depending on your device.
Does the compressed PDF have a watermark?
No. FileMagic Pro never adds watermarks to your files.
Is my PDF uploaded to your server?
No. Your PDF never leaves your device. All compression happens locally in your browser.
