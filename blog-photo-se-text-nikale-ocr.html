Photo Se Text
Kaise Nikale

Kisi bhi photo, scanned document, ya screenshot se text copy karne ka free tarika — Hindi aur English dono me.

Kabhi aapko kisi book page, notice board, ya scanned document ki photo click karni padi hai aur uska text type karne me time waste hua hai? OCR (Optical Character Recognition) technology se ye kaam sirf ek click me ho jata hai — photo se text seedha copy-paste ke liye ready mil jata hai.

OCR kya hai aur ye kaise kaam karta hai?

OCR ek technology hai jo image ke andar chhupe hue letters aur words ko pehchan kar unhe editable text me convert kar deti hai. Matlab aap photo ko directly Word document, WhatsApp message, ya kahin bhi paste karne layak text me badal sakte hain.

Photo se text nikalne ke 3 steps

  1. OCR tool kholo — browser me seedha kaam karta hai, koi app download nahi chahiye.
  2. Photo ya scanned image upload karo — file device se hi select karo.
  3. Text copy karo — kuch second me text nikal ke aa jata hai, seedha copy kar sakte ho.

Konsi languages support hoti hain?

Ye tool Hindi, English, Urdu aur Marathi — chaaron languages ka text pehchanta hai. Matlab agar aapki photo me Hindi ka koi notice, letter, ya form hai, to bhi text seedha nikal sakte ho.

Best results ke liye tips

  • Photo clear aur well-lit honi chahiye, blurry photo se accuracy kam hoti hai
  • Photo seedhi (straight angle se) khichi ho, tedhi photo se text galat pehchana ja sakta hai
  • Printed/typed text par accuracy sabse zyada hoti hai, handwriting par thodi kam ho sakti hai

FAQs

Kya ye Hindi text bhi pehchan sakta hai?
Haan, Hindi, English, Urdu aur Marathi — chaaron languages support karta hai.
Handwriting se bhi text nikal sakte hain kya?
Print/typed text ke liye sabse accurate hai. Handwriting me accuracy likhawat ki clarity par depend karti hai.
Photo kahin upload hoti hai kya?
Nahi, poora process aapke browser me hi hota hai, image kabhi server par nahi jaati.
