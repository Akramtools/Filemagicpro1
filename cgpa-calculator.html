Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert CGPA to percentage?
The most common formula used by CBSE and many Indian universities is Percentage = (CGPA - 0.5) × 10. For example, a CGPA of 8.5 converts to 80%.
Is the formula same for all universities?
No. CBSE uses (CGPA - 0.5) × 10, but universities like VTU, Anna University and others may use a different multiplier such as 9.5 or 9.4. Always check your university's official formula for official documents.
How do I convert percentage to CGPA?
Using the CBSE formula in reverse, CGPA = (Percentage ÷ 10) + 0.5. For example, 80% converts to 8.5 CGPA.
Is this accurate for official use?
This tool gives a quick estimate using standard formulas. For transcripts, admissions, or official certificates, always confirm the exact conversion formula with your school or university.
What is a good CGPA score in India?
A CGPA of 8 and above is generally considered excellent, 7 to 8 is very good, and 6 to 7 is considered average in most Indian grading systems, though standards vary by institution and course.
Does CGPA to percentage matter for job applications?
Yes, many companies and further education applications ask for percentage rather than CGPA, so converting accurately using your institution's official formula is important when filling forms.
Can I use this for SGPA to percentage conversion?
SGPA (semester GPA) and CGPA (cumulative GPA) use the same conversion formula in most cases, so you can use this calculator for SGPA to percentage conversion as well.
